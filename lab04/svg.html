About This Image

This image is a depiction of a bluebird. One of my favorite hobbies is birdwatching. In the spirit of natural beauty, there is something wonderful about hearing a bird's song after a long day to welcome you home. Click the thumbnail to see the full-size image.

Why SVG Format?

SVGs scale without loss because they are vector-based, so they don't lose quality when resized like a raster image. They also support full transparency, which makes it easy to blend this image into the background.
